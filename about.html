About RTM SOFT

RTM SOFT COMPANY LIMITED is a software company in Maha Sarakham, Thailand. We design, build and publish our own apps for iPhone and iPad, and distribute all of them through the App Store.

What we make

Most of our work is compatibility software: apps that let people run games and programs they already own on the iPhone and iPad they already carry. RTMHub runs visual novels and lightweight PC games. RTM-EMU runs PC and PlayStation 2 games, with custom controls and live translation.

We also make smaller tools for language and video: apps that turn what's on your screen into vocabulary practice, generate and translate subtitles in real time, and caption short videos for social media.

How we work

On your device

Our compatibility apps run software locally. They don't stream games from a server.

Bring your own content

We don't include, sell or download game files. You use software, ROMs and disc images that you legally own.

No ads

Our main apps are free to download, carry no ads and are funded by optional subscriptions.

Built for many languages

RTMHub and RTM-EMU are localized into 18 languages, and RTM-EMU can translate in-game text live.
