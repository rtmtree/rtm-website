Support

Answers to common questions about our apps. If you can't find what you need, contact us.

All apps

How do I get a refund?

The App Store handles all purchases and subscriptions, so refunds go through Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose the subscription and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and use the restore option on the subscription screen.

RTM-EMU

Where do I get games?

RTM-EMU doesn't include or download games. Use PC games, ROMs and disc images that you legally own, and import them into the app from your device.

A game runs slowly. What can I try?

In the emulator settings, turn on the fast CPU profile, turn off HD graphics, or turn on frame skip. Close other apps first. Newer iPhone and iPad models run demanding games best.

How do I connect a controller?

Pair an MFi, PlayStation or Xbox controller in Settings > Bluetooth, then open RTM-EMU. The app detects it automatically.

How does live translation work?

Tap the globe icon while playing. RTM-EMU reads the text on screen and shows a translation on top of the game. The free tier includes 100 translations a day; RTM Pro removes the limit.

RTMHub

Which games work?

RTMHub is designed for visual novels and lightweight 2D PC games. Large 3D games usually need more power than an iPhone or iPad can provide through compatibility software.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. Once a game is installed, it runs entirely on your device.
