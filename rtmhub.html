RTMHub

Visual novels and lightweight PC games, playable natively on iPhone and iPad.

View on the App Store

Free download. Requires iOS 15 or later.

What it does

RTMHub brings the freedom of desktop gaming to iPhone and iPad. It's built on open-source emulation and compatibility technology, including Wine and Wasm-Webport, so you can install and run your own software on Apple devices, including simple PC games that you legally own.

Our goal is to make visual novels and lightweight PC games playable natively on iOS hardware, without streaming and without external game servers. Everything runs on your device.

Features

Runs on your device

Games run locally on your iPhone or iPad. There's no cloud streaming, so you can play offline once a game is installed.

Visual novels first

Tuned for 2D games and visual novels, which work well with touch and with the screen sizes of iPhone and iPad.

Bring your own games

Install games you already own from your own files. RTMHub doesn't include, sell or download game files.

18 languages

The app is localized into English, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Spanish, French, German and more.
