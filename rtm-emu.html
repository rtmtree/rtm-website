RTM-EMU

A multi-system emulator for iPhone and iPad, built to feel like a modern app.

View on the App Store

Free download with optional RTM Pro subscription. Requires iOS 15 or later.

What it does

RTM-EMU runs PC games and PlayStation 2 games natively on iPhone and iPad. Point-and-click adventures, RPGs, indie titles and older Windows games run on the device, with no jailbreak, no sideloading and no PC required.

A drag-and-hold quick-launch wheel picks the emulator you want. Hold a segment for a second to make it your default, and the app opens straight into it next time.

Features

Two emulators in one app

A PC compatibility layer and a PlayStation 2 core, with one launcher for both. Users vote on the next system we add.

Controls that fit your hand

On-screen gamepads with several layouts. Drag, resize and mix buttons, analog sticks, mouse pads and labelled keys. Two on-screen controllers for local co-op.

Hardware controllers

Connect MFi, PlayStation or Xbox controllers for full physical controls.

Live in-game translation

Tap the globe icon to read the on-screen text and overlay a translation in your language, for games you can't otherwise read.

Save states

Save and load anywhere in PS2 games, and in PC games with RTM Pro.

Performance controls

A fast CPU profile for demanding games, an HD graphics option, frame skip, an FPS overlay and a click-only mouse mode for point-and-click games.

RTM Pro

RTM-EMU is free to download and has no ads. An optional RTM Pro subscription, $2.99 a month in the US, unlocks:

  • Save and load states in PC games
  • HD graphics on every emulator
  • Advanced configuration controls
  • A second on-screen controller and the custom controller editor
  • Unlimited translation (the free tier includes 100 a day)

The subscription is billed through your Apple Account. You can cancel any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
